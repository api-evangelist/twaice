# TWAICE

> TWAICE (TWAICE Technologies GmbH) is a Munich-based battery analytics software company. Its cloud platform turns BMS data from battery energy storage systems (BESS) and electric-vehicle fleets into predictive insights for performance management, warranty tracking, safety monitoring, digital commissioning, and BESS reporting, alongside a library of battery simulation models. TWAICE serves BESS asset owners and operators, IPPs, utilities, automotive and bus/truck OEMs, and battery manufacturers. The company is ISO 27001 certified and holds the automotive TISAX label. TWAICE does not publish a public API or developer program; the platform is a closed SaaS accessed by customers.
